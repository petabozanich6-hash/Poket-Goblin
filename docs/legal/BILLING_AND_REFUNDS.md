# Billing, Cancellation and Refund Policy

**Effective date:** 17 October 2026

Pocket Goblin is operated by Peta Bozanich, trading as Pocket Goblin (ABN 54 938 519 819). This policy explains how Premium subscriptions and refunds work alongside the [Terms of Service](/terms).

## Free means free

The Free plan has no trial countdown, no payment-card requirement and no automatic upgrade. You are charged only if you choose a Premium subscription and complete Stripe Checkout.

## Premium subscription charges

Premium can be purchased monthly or annually. Standard Australian base prices are **A$9.95/month** or **A$99/year**, unless a valid promotion applies. Stripe may present a supported local currency. Before paying, Stripe Checkout displays your exact recurring amount, currency, interval and any applicable taxes. Check those details before confirming.

A recurring subscription renews at the end of each billing period until you cancel it. Stripe processes payments and makes receipts and payment-method management available through the customer portal.

## How to cancel

Open **Settings → Plan → Manage billing**, then use Stripe's customer portal to cancel your subscription. You can do this at any time; you do not need to email us to ask permission. If you cannot access your account or the portal, contact **support@pocketgoblin.org** and we will help you manage the subscription.

Cancellation stops future renewal charges. Premium access normally continues until the end of the current paid period. After that, your account changes to Free and existing data is retained, subject to Free plan limits. Uninstalling the app, logging out or stopping use does not by itself cancel billing.

## Refunds and billing problems

A change of mind or unused time does not automatically entitle you to a partial refund for an active subscription period. If you think you have been charged incorrectly, paid twice, could not access the service you paid for, or received a service that did not meet legal guarantees, contact us. Include the account email, charge date and a description of the issue; **do not send card numbers or security codes**.

We will investigate, explain the outcome and arrange an appropriate remedy where required. Refunds, when granted, are generally returned through Stripe to the original payment method; the time it takes to appear depends on your payment provider.

**Your legal rights remain intact.** Australian Consumer Law guarantees and any other mandatory rights applicable to your purchase take priority over a change-of-mind policy. Where the law entitles you to cancel, receive a refund for the unused portion or obtain another remedy, we will honour that entitlement.

## Promotions and pricing changes

Promotion codes may have eligibility, expiry and redemption limits. An offer described as applying to the **first invoice** discounts that invoice only, whether the customer selects monthly or annual billing; later renewals return to the otherwise applicable recurring price unless an offer expressly provides a further discount. Read the Stripe Checkout breakdown before completing a payment. Time-limited Free Premium giveaways do not create a paid subscription or automatically start renewal charges. We will communicate material changes to recurring prices before they apply, in accordance with applicable law.

## Help

For a billing problem, contact **support@pocketgoblin.org**. Signed-in users can also use **Messages to Goblin HQ** for general support.
