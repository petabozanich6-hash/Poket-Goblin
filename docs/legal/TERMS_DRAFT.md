## The service

Pocket Goblin helps you organise everyday life: tasks, reminders, notes,
shopping, calendar entries and paperwork. It never pays bills, submits forms
or contacts anyone on your behalf.

When you tell the Goblin something clear ("I need milk", "remind me to call
Mum on Thursday"), it adds or changes that item straight away and offers
**Undo**. Changes to appointments, and moving several things at once, always
ask you first. Confident suggestions from a document you upload may also be
added automatically. In Settings you can switch on **"Ask me before the
Goblin changes anything"**.

**Always check important dates and amounts against the original document.**
The Goblin can misread things.

## Plans

- **Free** — permanently free, no trial and no card needed. It includes:
  - 10 active tasks
  - 5 notes
  - 3 active reminders
  - 1 shopping list (no limit on items)
  - 3 AI-powered requests a day (smart sorting and document reading; the
    built-in rule-based Goblin keeps working after that)
  - 2 document reads a month
  - 10 MB of kept documents
  - overwhelm help, calendar and everyday organising

  Reaching a limit never deletes anything: finish or remove something to
  make room, or upgrade.

- **Premium**, monthly or annual, with no free trial. It includes:
  - no caps on active tasks, notes or reminders
  - 40 AI-powered requests a day

  - 1 GB of kept documents

  and amount shown on the Stripe checkout page are what you pay.**

  any time from Settings → Plan; Premium continues until the end of the paid
  period, then the account returns to Free with everything kept.
