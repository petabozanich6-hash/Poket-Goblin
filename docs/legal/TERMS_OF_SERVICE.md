## Free and Premium plans

**Free — Meet Your Goblin** is an ongoing free plan, not a trial. It does not require a payment card or turn into a paid subscription automatically. Current default Free allowances include 10 active tasks, 5 notes, 3 active reminders, one shopping list, 3 AI-powered requests per day, 2 document-processing attempts per month and 10 MB of kept documents. Some everyday rule-based functions remain available when an AI limit is reached.

**Premium — Unleash the Goblin** offers expanded capacity, including uncapped active tasks, notes and reminders, a default allowance of 40 AI-powered requests per day, more document processing and 1 GB of kept documents. Processing, storage, security and reasonable-use limits still apply. Your current plan and applicable allowances are shown in **Settings → Plan**; some operational limits may be adjusted in Goblin HQ.

When an allowance is reached, you may need to finish or remove items, wait for a reset or change plans. Reaching a plan limit does not erase your existing entries. If Premium ends, your records remain, but you may need to reduce usage before creating additional items within Free limits.
